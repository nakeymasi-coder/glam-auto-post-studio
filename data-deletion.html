🦋 Delete Your Data

Glam Auto-Post Studio · A Glam Hustle Hub tool

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your data is yours. Here is exactly how to delete everything Glam Auto-Post Studio holds about you.

Option 1 — Delete everything inside the Studio (fastest)

  1. Open Glam Auto-Post Studio.
  2. Go to the My Voice tab.
  3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete everything.
  4. Confirm. Your posts, drafts, proof log, saved handles, and voice profile are erased from your browser immediately and completely.

Because the Studio stores your data in your own browser on your own device, this removes all of it. There is no copy on our side to delete afterwards.

Option 2 — Clear your browser storage

You can also remove the Studio's data through your browser's settings by clearing site data for this website. That deletes the same stored content.

Connected platform accounts

If you connected a social media account when platform connections are available, you can disconnect it in two places, and either one works:

• In the Studio, on the Accounts tab — tap Remove next to the platform.
• In that platform's own settings, under connected apps / permissions — remove Glam Auto-Post Studio.

Want a copy first?

Before deleting, you can download everything: in the Studio, go to My Voice → Your data → Download my data to save a file with your posts, accounts, and voice profile.

Still need help?

If anything above does not work for you, reach Glam Hustle Hub through glamhustlehub.com and ask for help deleting your Glam Auto-Post Studio data. We will walk you through it.
