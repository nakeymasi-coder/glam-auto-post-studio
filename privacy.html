🦋 Privacy Policy

Glam Auto-Post Studio · A Glam Hustle Hub tool

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Glam Auto-Post Studio ("the Studio") is a content planning tool from Glam Hustle Hub. This page explains, in plain language, what information the Studio handles and what happens to it.

What the Studio stores

The Studio stores the information you put into it:

• The posts, captions, drafts, and schedules you create
• Your live post links, which form your proof log
• Your "voice" profile — your tone, signature phrases, a sample caption, your hashtags, and your call to action
• The social media handles you save on the Accounts page

This information is stored in your own browser, on your own device, using your browser's local storage. It is not uploaded to us when you use the Studio this way.

Connected accounts

Live auto-posting requires each social platform to approve the Studio app. When platform connections become available and you choose to connect an account, the Studio will ask that platform for permission to post on your behalf. Access tokens are used only to carry out the posting and scheduling you set up. You can disconnect an account at any time, in the Studio and in your account settings on that platform.

What we never do

• We never sell your data. Ever.
• We never post anything you did not create and schedule yourself.
• We never ask for your social media passwords. Platform connections use each platform's own secure sign-in, never a password typed into the Studio.

Cookies and tracking

The Studio uses your browser's local storage to remember your content on your device. The Studio does not run advertising trackers.

Deleting your data

Everything stored on your device can be deleted by you, at any time, in one tap — see How to delete your data. Because your content lives on your device, deleting it there removes it completely.

Questions

Questions about this policy or your data: reach Glam Hustle Hub through glamhustlehub.com.
