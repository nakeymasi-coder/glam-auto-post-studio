🦋 Government Requests Policy

Glam Auto-Post Studio · A Glam Hustle Hub tool

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Glam Hustle Hub LLC ("we") handles requests from government agencies, law enforcement, or other public authorities for user data connected to Glam Auto-Post Studio ("the Studio").

We review every request for legality

Every request is reviewed before anything is disclosed. We require a valid legal process — such as a subpoena, court order, or warrant — that is properly issued, in the correct jurisdiction, and specific about the data it covers. Requests that do not meet that standard are not answered with data.

We challenge unlawful or overbroad requests

If a request is unlawful, overbroad, or seeks more data than the law allows, we will challenge it, seek to narrow it, or decline it, and we will use the legal channels available to us to do so.

We disclose the minimum required

When a valid request must be answered, we disclose only the minimum data the law requires — nothing more. We never provide voluntary or bulk access to user data.

We document every request

Every request we receive, our review of it, and our response are documented and kept on record, including the legal basis relied on.

About Studio data

The Studio stores a user's own content in that user's own browser, on their own device. We do not hold copies of users' posts, schedules, or connected-account content on our servers, which limits what could ever be requested from us. Access tokens for connected accounts are used only to carry out the posting and scheduling the user set up.

Notifying users

Where the law allows it, we will notify an affected user about a request for their data before disclosing anything, so they have the opportunity to respond or challenge it themselves.

Questions

Questions about this policy: reach Glam Hustle Hub through glamhustlehub.com.
